The findingThe Voynich Manuscript is read backwards, and the sixth entry from its last page is the first word of Genesis

The Voynich Manuscript — Beinecke Rare Book & Manuscript Library MS 408, about 240 vellum pages in an unknown script, the vellum carbon-dated 1404–1438, undeciphered since it surfaced — is read from the last page to the first, each line right to left. On that reading — and on no other — entry 6 of the starred text carries raw letter sum 913 = BERESHIT, with 203, the second word of Genesis 1:1, following in the positional coordinate that only the mirror produces.

The same reversal is what made the Rongorongo tablets significant in Paper III of START: read forward they give null on every tablet; read backward, two turn significant at p ≈ 0.03.

MethodThree habits, inherited from Paper III

  1. Computed, not eyeballed. Bearings and distances are great-circle values; sky positions come from an ephemeris with the observer's parallax applied and Earth rotation taken from the catalogue's own ΔT.
  2. Baselines before applause. A hit rate means nothing until the chance rate is known. The figure-order tests report how many placements were tried.
  3. Negative results in the same type size. The Genesis verse fit failed. It is on its own page, at the same size as the successes.
