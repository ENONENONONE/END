IV · the Voynich zodiac figures

299 figures, 294 stars

Every figure on the Voynich Manuscript's twelve zodiac pages, read by eye from the full-size Beinecke images: what is drawn, and the marks that break the pattern.

Wallet: bc1qd8wcrsmkk0ghegf5t2jsezg2ve7rtx6vvc79lx

Ten readers went through the Voynich Manuscript's twelve zodiac pages figure by figure, each from the full-size Beinecke image and the transliteration's label list, recording what is drawn and not what it means.

For every figure: clothed or naked, what is on the head, whether she stands in a barrel or on one, her posture and facing, which arm holds the star, whether the star hangs on a string, how many points it has, its paint, and anything else drawn with her. Every page was checked against the transcribers' count.

Barrels and clothing fall away in one direction. Every Pisces and Aries figure has a container; Taurus keeps them on one page and loses them on the other; Gemini has three standing on barrels; after Gemini there is one tub in all of Virgo and nothing else. Clothing follows the same line — 16 clothed in Aries, 12 in Taurus, 4 in Gemini, none at all from Cancer onward.

The light page of each doubled sign is the clothed and painted one; the dark page is the bare one.
