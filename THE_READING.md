# HOW THE VOYNICH MANUSCRIPT IS TO BE READ

The procedure, stated so it can be followed or broken.

---

## 1. Direction

**Open at the last page and read toward the first. Within each line, read right to left.**

- The starred quire runs f116v, then f116r back to f103r. The rest of the book follows in the same backward order: pharmaceutical, herbal, rosettes, bathing, zodiac, astronomical, herbal, f1r.
- Within a line, take the glyphs from the right end. Letter sums do not change, so a cell is the same either way; the positional part (the φ coordinate) does change, and it is the φ part that carries the reading.
- Lines within an entry run top to bottom. This is the one part not settled: read bottom to top as well and entry 6 gives 192 instead of 203.

**Why.** The zodiac quire in binding order runs the Sun's year forward, Pisces to Sagittarius. Read back to front it runs the order of the ages, ending on Pisces — where the equinox stood in 1420. Precession runs against the year, so a record of precession runs against the book.

The same reversal is what made the Rongorongo tablets significant in Paper III: standard order gives null, reversed-recto gives p = 0.029 and p = 0.036 on two independent tablets.

---

## 2. Scale

**A figure is a degree of the Sun's path. Twelve signs of thirty figures is 360 — one whole turn.**

Read the turn at either scale and the drawing supports both:

| Read as | One figure | One sign | The whole wheel |
|---|---|---|---|
| The year | 1 day | 30 days | 365 days |
| Precession | 71.6 years | 2,148 years = one dlog ray | 25,772 years |

The twelve signs are the twelve rays of GF(257) — dlog mod 12, twenty-one cells each, 21 = F(8). This is START's result, written before the manuscript was opened.

---

## 3. Anchor

**The book dates itself. Do not bring a date to it.**

Two tests, run separately, land in the same decade:

- The five figures that hold no star: in **1408** Gemini 13 sits on the new Moon, Cancer 10 one day before it, Virgo 22 on the full Moon. **1427** repeats this within 2°.
- f103r's nineteen stars with seven painted red: read from the foot upward, they fall on the nineteen-year cycle's seven long years.

And 27 × 257 = 6939 days against 6939.75 in nineteen Julian years. The field's modulus and the Metonic cycle agree to within a day, so dates one cycle apart share a cell. The nineteen-year count and the field keep step on their own.

Prague, 50.1° N, in the frame of date: obliquity 23.5146°, the longest day 10φ hours exactly, sunrise swinging 51.54° to 128.46°.

---

## 4. What the text is

**The text of a passage is the path of the Sun for that passage.**

Measured, not assumed: over the ten surviving signs, mean label length against the length of the day at Prague gives r = +0.645; mean letter value gives r = −0.858. Long-day signs carry more a i r n m l h c; short-day signs more e d y k o. The line is hours = 13.50 + 7.32 × index.

So a longer word is a longer course of the Sun, and the lettering itself is the measure. The lost Capricorn would run 5.21 letters to a label and Aquarius 5.58.

---

## 5. What the marks are

Of 299 figures, 294 hold a star. The marks that break the pattern are the data:

- **Five hold no star**: Gemini 13, Cancer 10, Cancer 18, Virgo 22, Sagittarius 18.
- **Four are crowned**: Cancer 27, Leo 24, Libra 7, Scorpio 14.
- **Stars on a string** fall away across the book: Aries dark 13 of 15, Pisces 26 of 29, Cancer 18, Scorpio 6, Libra none.
- **Barrels and clothing** fall away in the same direction: every Aries and Pisces figure has a container; after Gemini there is one tub in all of Virgo and nothing else.
- Eclipses Prague could see land on figures whose star is marked and no other: 1408's on the one blotted star, 1415's total on the one star drawn in solid paint with no outline, 1424's on the one half figure.

---

## 6. What the answer must satisfy

Any proposed decipherment has to clear what has already been measured:

- Letter entropy 2.12–2.35 bits, against 3.04–3.60 for ten candidate languages.
- 2.7 to 4.4 standard deviations from every one of those languages on key-free measures, when they sit 0.6 to 2.7 from each other.
- No `a` followed by `u` anywhere, against 4,279 `a` followed by two strokes.
- Stroke runs closed by the hook stop at four: 137 of one, 1,719 of two, 4,131 of three, 165 of four, one of five — scribal numerals j, ij, iij, iiij. Read as powers of four: 4, 16, 64, 256, 253, 241, 193, 1.
- Rare glyphs keep strict company: b always last after e (15 of 15), u always last or alone (8 of 8).
- And it must fail where the field failed: no additive glyph values make the entries, lines or words equal the verses of Genesis in order.

## 7. What is still open

1. Whether lines inside an entry run down only (203) or up as well (192).
2. Whether the rings of a zodiac page are read outward, inward, or by the clock. Every result above takes the transcribers' list order and has not been tested against another.
3. Why three of the five starless figures do not fall on a new Moon.
4. The two lost signs. Folio 74 carried Capricorn and Aquarius, so mid-December to early February has no figure to land on.
5. The centre words — mars, aberil, may, yony, iollet, augst, octebre, novebre — are month names in a Romance hand. Whose hand, and whether they were added later, is not settled here.
