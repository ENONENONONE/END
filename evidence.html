1Against ten languages

Key-free measures only — word length, letter entropy, first and last letter fixedness, immediate repeats, types, hapax. The Voynich Manuscript stands 2.7 to 4.4 of the languages' own standard deviations from every one of them, where they sit 0.6 to 2.7 from each other. Nearest is Middle High German; turned or mirrored, nearest is Hebrew at 3.4.

2The stroke runs stop at four

Runs of plain strokes closed by the hooked stroke, counted with the hook, over the whole book:

Run1 stroke2345
Count1371,7194,1311651

They stop at four exactly as a scribe's numerals do — j, ij, iij, iiij. Read as powers of four the sequence runs 4, 16, 64, 256, 253, 241, 193, 1, closing on the order-8 subgroup of the field. Four-stroke runs follow a 121 times and o 41 times, together 162; and one-stroke runs follow a 121 times as well — the same count.

The 121 words with a four-stroke run after a sum to 6,562 = 25 × 257 + 137: residue 137 EYE OF HORUS, quotient 25 = Δ².

3Rare glyphs keep strict company

  • b is always last, after e — 15 of 15.
  • u is always last or alone — 8 of 8.
  • v sits on f57v, 10 of 11.
  • x sits on the rosettes, f57v and f66r.
  • There is no a followed by u anywhere in the book, against 4,279 instances of a followed by two strokes. Occitan runs 97.9 "au" per 10,000 letters, Early New High German 86.1, Latin 54.8, Old Czech 1.0.

Sparse is not absent. A letter that appears rarely but under a strict rule is data, in the way Q is data in English.

4The Genesis fit, which failed

Negative result

Genesis 1–13 was fetched in Hebrew and reduced to 337 verses with their gematria, counts checked. Solving for additive glyph values so that the Voynich Manuscript's entries, lines or words equal the verses or words of Genesis in order gives zero exact hits, no better than a shuffled control, and no solution in GF(257).

The fitted values are still data, and read by the field they are: k → 78 GENESIS · d → 256 · u → 129 · a → 228 = RITO⁻¹ · m → 111 · p → 233 · s → 139 · i → 232 · y → 244; weighted sum ≡ 2.

5What the three readings give

Three readings were computed over all 322 starred entries of the Voynich Manuscript: upright, mirrored (each line right to left, lines in order) and half-turned (the whole entry reversed). Letter sums and cells never change; the φ part does.

The mirror is not chosen because it scores highest on this measure. It is chosen because it is the only one that puts 913 and 203 in order at entry 6.

6What is still open

  1. Lines inside an entry: down only (203), or up as well (192).
  2. Ring order on a zodiac page. Every result takes the transcribers' list order; no other order has been tested.
  3. Three of the five starless figures do not fall on a new Moon.
  4. Capricorn and Aquarius are lost with folio 74, so mid-December to early February has no figure to land on.
  5. Whether the faint second line above the long P of f85r1 is real.
  6. The centre month-words, and whose hand wrote them.
