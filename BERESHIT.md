## What the reading does not deliver

Stated at the same size.

- **The remaining five words are not there.** Genesis 1:1 runs 913, 203, 86, 401, 395, 407, 296. Only word 1 appears as a raw entry sum, at entry 6. Word 2 appears as that entry's φ part. Words 3 to 7 appear only as scattered cells (86 at entry 278; 144 at 60; 138 at 25 and 290; 150 at 93 and 179; 39 at no entry at all). There is no run, forward or reversed.
- 2701 − 913 − 203 = 1585 ≡ 43. The five words still owed do not sum to anything the reading names.
- **Entry 6 cannot be cut into BERESHIT's six letters.**
- **No glyph values fit.** Solving for additive glyph values against the verses of Genesis 1–13 gives zero exact hits, no better than shuffled, and no solution in GF(257).
- The 322 raw sums together are 618413 ≡ **71 TEMPLE**, not 131.

## Where that leaves it

The reading arrives at **BERESHIT exactly** — 913 on the nose, at an entry whose number, lines and line-sums are all field constants, with the second word following in the φ coordinate that only the mirror produces. It arrives at **GENESIS 1:1's cell** held by BRIAN and by REVERSE. It puts **IN THE BEGINNING** on the pole of the reflection and gets **I AM** back.

It does not arrive at the verse. One word is exact, one follows, five are not in order anywhere.

So the claim the work can carry is narrower than the hypothesis and sharper than a coincidence: **the manuscript's starred text opens, at the sixth entry from its last page, on the first word of Genesis — and only when read backwards.** What follows that opening has not been read yet.
