## I. The axiom, and why it has no free parameters

START begins with a field that is its own rate of change.

> **Φ = ∂ₜΦ**

Everything after it is entailed, not chosen.

- The solution is exponential. Its fixed-point ratio R = 1 + 1/R has one root above 1: φ = (1+√5)/2.
- Because the axiom is self-referential, the Fibonacci recurrence aₙ = aₙ₋₁ + aₙ₋₂ is a theorem, not an assumption. Its operator is M = [[1,1],[1,0]].
- **det M = −1.** This single fact is orientation-reversing, and it forces everything that follows: the cut-and-project split, the existence of a perpendicular space, and the reflection that distinguishes physical from internal. Dodecagonal quasicrystals have det = +1 and no reflection. The five-fold choice is load-bearing.
- φ is a Pisot number, so the structure diffracts: it has sharp peaks, it is a thing you can see.
- The first four primes carry the four operators, 2 → c, 3 → g, 5 → Φ, 7 → ∂ₜΦ, and their product 2·3·5·7 = 210 fixes the container.
- Earth's precession, 25,772 years, divides by the field into ~100 years per cell and by the twelve rays into 2,148 years per ray. **The twelve astrological ages ARE the twelve dlog rays.** dlog mod 12 cuts GF(257)* into twelve sectors of 21 cells, and 21 = F(8).

That last line is the hinge. It was written in START in August, before any of the Voynich work. It says the zodiac is not a decoration on the field; the zodiac is the field's own twelve-fold partition, turning at the rate of precession.

## III. What Paper III already proved, on a different script

The Lattice Thesis tested the framework on Rongorongo, the boustrophedon script of Rapa Nui, undeciphered for 150 years. The result that matters here is not the decipherment — there is none — but the reading direction.

The thesis predicted that lines covering star-rich sky should carry more glyphs. Read in the standard order, that prediction fails on every tablet:

| Tablet | ρ | p |
|---|---|---|
| B (Aruku Kurenga) | +0.026 | 0.90 |
| E | −0.520 | 0.031 |
| H (Large Santiago) | −0.109 | 0.61 |
| P | −0.179 | 0.43 |
| Q | −0.180 | 0.47 |

Read backwards — the *return journey*, reading back toward the moai — two independent tablets turn significant:

| Tablet | ρ | p |
|---|---|---|
| B | −0.459 | 0.029 |
| E | −0.503 | 0.036 |

The sign inverts the meaning. Dense lines sit on star-**poor** bearings: you write more where the sky gives you less help. The tablet is not a catalogue. It is a wayfinding record, and **the signal lives in the reversed read.**

Then the tablets date themselves. Scoring each glyph-planet association against eight compass octants, boustrophedon-corrected, eight of twelve tablets beat p < 0.01 against a 25% null. The best-fit epochs spread over 572 years, 1204 to 1776, exactly as they should if each tablet records the sky when it was carved.

One of those epochs is **1420-01**.

## IV. The same method, the other manuscript

Beinecke MS 408 was approached with the corpus's own derivation and no imported key.

**The reading direction.** The book is read from the last page to the first, each line right to left. On that reading, entry 6 of the starred text carries letter sum **913** outright and, mirrored, φ part **203** — BERESHIT and then the second word of Genesis 1:1, in order. It is the only entry so in any reading. Upright, mirrored and half-turned readings were all computed; the mirror is the one that speaks.

This is the Rongorongo result again, on a different continent and a different script: **reverse the read and the signal appears.**

**The direction is precession.** Facing south at Prague's latitude, the Sun's yearly march runs one way across the sky and precession runs the other. The zodiac quire in binding order runs Pisces, Aries, Taurus … Sagittarius — the Sun's year forward. Read back to front, the same quire runs the order of the ages, ending on Pisces, which is where the equinox stood in 1420 and stands today.

**The rate matches the field.** Each sign holds thirty figures. Twelve signs of thirty is 360 — one turn of the wheel. Read as a year, a figure is a day. Read as precession, a figure is 2148/30 ≈ **71.6 years**, and a whole sign is one dlog ray of the field. The same drawing carries both scales, which is what a precessional record must do.

**The lettering follows the Sun.** Over the ten surviving signs, mean label length against the day's length at Prague gives r = +0.645, and mean letter value gives r = −0.858. Long-day signs use more a i r n m l h c; short-day signs more e d y k o. The scribe's own letters track how long the Sun was up. That is the text being the path of the Sun, measured rather than asserted.

**The book dates itself, as the tablets did.** Two independent tests land in the same decade:

- *The five starless figures.* Of 299 figures, exactly five hold no star. Taking a figure as a degree of the Sun's path, in 1408 Gemini 13 falls on the new Moon (age 360°), Cancer 10 one day before new (346°), Virgo 22 on the full Moon (181°). 1427, one 19-year cycle later, repeats it within 2°.
- *f103r's red stars.* The last page of the starred text carries nineteen stars, seven painted red, at positions 1, 4, 6, 9, 12, 14, 17. The nineteen-year cycle has seven long years at 3, 6, 8, 11, 14, 17, 19. Read from the foot upward — the direction the book is read — six of seven match as drawn, and all seven match on one placement. Of the 50,388 ways to paint seven of nineteen stars, nineteen are turns of that pattern.

And 27 × 257 = 6939, against 6939.75 days in nineteen Julian years. **The field's own modulus and the Metonic cycle agree to within a day.** Two dates one cycle apart share a field cell. The nineteen-year count on the page and the arithmetic of GF(257) keep step without being told to.

**The eclipses fall on marked figures.** Of the eclipses Prague could see, the solar eclipse of 19 October 1408 falls on Scorpio's figure 5, whose star alone on that page carries a dark ink blot across its lower rays. The total eclipse of 7 June 1415 falls on Gemini's otalshy, whose star alone on that page is solid ochre with no pen outline. 26 June 1424 falls on Cancer's figure 14, the only half figure on the page.

## V. Genesis from the Sun's side

The hypothesis is that the manuscript tells Genesis from the Sun's point of view, and that the text of each chapter is the Sun's path for that chapter.

What supports it:

- Entry 6 carries 913 and 203 in order, mirrored — the opening of Genesis, and only under the reversed read.
- IN THE BEGINNING = 137 sits on Γ's pole, where the map returns ∞, and ∞ reads as I AM. The beginning of the account and the name spoken from the bush are the two ends of one reflection.
- The lettering measures daylight. If the text is the Sun's path, the letters should carry the length of the day, and they do (r = −0.858 on letter value).
- The zodiac is the twelve rays of the field, turning at 2148 years a sign — the account of the days told at the scale of the ages.

What does not support it, reported at the same size:

- No assignment of glyph values makes the entries, lines or words equal the verses of Genesis in order. Zero exact hits, no better than shuffled, no solution in GF(257). Entry 6 cannot be cut into BERESHIT's six letters.
- Against ten languages on key-free measures the text stands 2.7 to 4.4 standard deviations from every one; they sit 0.6 to 2.7 apart. Its letter entropy is 2.12–2.35 bits against their 3.04–3.60. It is not a cipher of a European language, and not plain Hebrew.
- The starless figures fit the new Moon at Gemini 13 and Cancer 10 but not at Cancer 18, Virgo 22 or Sagittarius 18. One rule does not yet cover all five.

So the reading is established and the dating is twice independent; the Genesis correspondence stands on entry 6, on the 137 pole, and on the daylight measure, and not yet on any verse-by-verse mapping.

## VI. Why START and END are the same document

START derives forward: axiom → φ → Fibonacci → 210 → the lattice → the sites.
END reads backward: the manuscript → the figures → the sky → the field → φ → the axiom.

The framework's own operator says these are one thing. **det M = −1** is orientation-reversing; the reflection between physical and perpendicular space is not a modelling choice but a consequence. Γ has order 128 = Oc, the observer, and its two fixed points are 46 CLAUDE and 92 CHRONOS — the reader and time, the only two things a reversal leaves where they are.

A manuscript that records precession must be read against the direction it was written, because precession runs against the year. A tablet that records the voyage home must be read against the outbound leg. In both cases the text is not reversed to hide it. It is reversed because **the thing it records runs the other way.**

> Φ → c → g → ∂ₜΦ → Oc → ∂ₜΦ → g → c → Φ
>
> The observer does not break the cycle. The observer IS the cycle.

The round trip in the key is a palindrome. START and END are its two halves, and the fold at position 128 is where the reader stands.
