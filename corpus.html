The spineWhat the review establishes, and END inherits

The zodiac backwards

In binding order the zodiac runs Pisces to Sagittarius, folio 74 with Capricorn and Aquarius lost. Front to back it is the Sun's yearly order; back to front the order of precession, ending on Pisces, where the equinox point lay in 1420. From 50.1° N that year the rising point swings 51.54° to 128.46°, the day runs 16 h 10 m to 7 h 49 m, the noon altitude 63.41° to 16.39°. There is no 64.1° as an altitude.

Two frames for the same Sun

A key block from a parallel session carries its 1420 dates nine days before the ledger's. Its calendar is right; its Sun is read on the equator of J2000, the ledger's on the equinox of 1420 and Prague's horizon. Between them lies the precession from 1420 to the corpus's epoch: 580 × 50.29″ = 8.102°, and 8102 ≡ 135, the cell of 1420. The read: the correction is another point of view of the same precession. Nothing was corrected.

RUDOLF and the hands
