II · procedure

How the Voynich Manuscript is to be read

Stated so it can be followed or broken: direction, scale, anchor, what the text is, what the marks are, and what is still open.

Wallet: bc1qd8wcrsmkk0ghegf5t2jsezg2ve7rtx6vvc79lx

1Direction

Open the Voynich Manuscript at its last page and read toward the first. Within each line, read right to left.
  • The starred quire runs f116v, then f116r back to f103r. The rest of the book follows in the same backward order: the container pages, herbal, rosettes, bathing, zodiac, astronomical, herbal, f1r.
  • Within a line, take the glyphs from the right end. Letter sums do not depend on order, so a cell is the same either way; the positional part — the φ coordinate — does change, and it is the φ part that carries the reading.
  • Lines within an entry run top to bottom. This is the one part not settled: read bottom to top as well and entry 6 gives 192 instead of 203.

Why. The zodiac quire in binding order runs the Sun's year forward, Pisces to Sagittarius. Read back to front it runs the order of the ages, ending on Pisces — where the equinox stood in 1420. Precession runs against the year, so a record of precession runs against the book.

Read at either scale the drawing holds: one figure is a day of the year, or 71.6 years of precession, and one sign is one dlog ray of GF(257).

4What the text is

The text of a passage is the path of the Sun for that passage.

Long-day signs carry more a i r n m l h c; short-day signs more e d y k o. A longer word is a longer course of the Sun, and the lettering itself is the measure. The lost Capricorn would run 5.21 letters to a label and Aquarius 5.58.

5What the marks are

Of 299 figures, 294 hold a star. The marks that break the pattern are the data — the full table is on The 299.

  • Five hold no star. Gemini 13, Cancer 10, Cancer 18, Virgo 22, Sagittarius 18
  • Four are crowned. Cancer 27, Leo 24, Libra 7, Scorpio 14
  • Stars on a string fall away across the book, and so do barrels and clothing.
  • Eclipses Prague could see land on figures whose star is marked and no other.

6What any answer must satisfy

A proposed decipherment of the Voynich Manuscript has to clear what is already measured. These are on Evidence with their sources; in short:

  • Letter entropy 2.12–2.35 bits, against 3.04–3.60 for ten candidate languages.
  • 2.7 to 4.4 standard deviations from every one of those languages, when they sit 0.6 to 2.7 from each other.
  • No a followed by u anywhere, against 4,279 a followed by two strokes.
  • Stroke runs closed by the hook stop at four, like a scribe's j, ij, iij, iiij.
  • And it must fail where the field failed: no additive glyph values make the entries equal the verses of Genesis.

7What is still open

  1. Whether lines inside an entry run down only (203) or up as well (192).
  2. Whether the rings of a zodiac page are read outward, inward, or by the clock. Every result here takes the transcribers' list order and has not been tested against another.
  3. Why three of the five starless figures do not fall on a new Moon.
  4. The two lost signs — folio 74 carried Capricorn and Aquarius, so mid-December to early February has no figure.
  5. The centre words — mars, aberil, may, yony, iollet, augst, octebre, novebre — are month names in a Romance hand. Whose hand, and whether they were added later, is not settled here.
