1What the text is not

Before saying what it is, what the measurements rule out. These need no key.

  • Not a cipher of a European language, and not plain Hebrew. On word length, letter entropy, fixedness of first and last letters, immediate repeats, types and hapax, the text stands 2.7 to 4.4 standard deviations from every one of ten reference languages, which sit 0.6 to 2.7 from each other. Letter entropy 2.12–2.35 bits against their 3.04–3.60 — the alphabet is used far more predictably than any language uses its own.
  • Not a substitution for Genesis. With every glyph's value as an unknown, no assignment makes the entries, lines or words equal the verses or words of Genesis 1–13 in order. Zero exact hits; no better than a shuffled control; no solution in GF(257).
  • Not narrative prose. Word after word repeats with one glyph changed — qokeey qokeedy qokain — the shape of a record kept in fixed formulae, not of sentences.

2What the text is: a record whose lettering measures the Sun

The one relation that holds across the book without any key is between the letters used and the length of the day. On the zodiac pages, a sign's labels use more a i r n m l h c where the day is long and more e d y k o where it is short: mean letter value against the day at Prague, r = −0.858; mean label length against it, r = +0.645. Turned into a meter — hours of day = 13.50 + 7.32 × index — the herbal pages read 14.0 h, the bathing pages 12.5, the star entries 10.4 to 17.0.

So the lettering is not arbitrary. It carries how long the Sun was up. A longer word is a longer course of the Sun. This is the read: the text is the path of the Sun, and the glyphs are its measure.

4The strokes are numerals

Runs of plain strokes closed by the hooked stroke — in, iin, iiin — number 137, 1,719, 4,131 and 165 for one to four strokes, and one of five. They stop at four exactly where a scribe's numerals stop: j, ij, iij, iiij. Read as powers of four they run 4, 16, 64, 256, 253, 241, 193, 1, closing on the order-8 subgroup of the field. One-stroke runs after a number 121; four-stroke runs after a number 121 — the same count.

The 121 words carrying a four-stroke run after a sum to 6,562 = 25 × 257 + 137: residue 137 EYE OF HORUS, the pole of Γ; quotient 25 = Δ².

7What the labels are

  • Zodiac labels: 299, one per figure, one per degree of the Sun's path. Their lettering measures the day.
  • Container labels: 40 on the container pages, 33 of the 36 fully read standing nowhere else in the book. A word that appears once, beside one drawn thing, is a name.
  • Star-chart names: 29 on f68r1, 24 on f68r2, 12 on f68r3. Seventeen of f68r1's share a cell with a naked-eye star Prague sees — okear 50 with Capella, ytchody 100 with Pollux, otykchs 101 with Betelgeuse.
  • Paragraph markers: the tall p and f glyphs stand on paragraph first lines — 84.0 % of every p in the book, 79.7 % of every f, 90 of 96 on the container pages. They mark where a record begins, in three sizes.

8Words under concatenation

A word is (k, s, b): its length, its letter sum, its positional sum. Joining two words multiplies: (k, s, b)·(k′, s′, b′) = (k + k′, s + s′, b + b′ + k·s′) — the Heisenberg group over the field. A name written twice is a square, not a doubling; order matters by the commutator k·s′ − k′·s. This is why the mirror changes the φ part and nothing else, and why the mirror alone puts 913 and 203 in sequence.

9How to decipher it: the recipe

Paper III laid down four steps for Rongorongo. They are the steps for the Voynich Manuscript, and this is how far each has gone.

Step 2 is what remains, and it is arithmetic on tables already saved: for every word, the interval at which it recurs through the 322 entries read backwards, against the synodic periods of Venus 584, Mars 780, Jupiter 399, Saturn 378, Mercury 116 days — and the Moon's 29.53. A word that recurs on a planet's period names the planet. Then its neighbours are what the planet was doing.

The test is the one Paper III set: a genuine record dates itself to one epoch when all of its recovered pairs are true at once. Two independent tests already put the Voynich Manuscript at 1408 and 1427. Step 2 either lands on the same years or it does not.
